Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy applies to these games: Hangry Settlers, Balloon Bringer, Nugget Scout, Hangry Flyer, Settled Steak Snake, and Dinner Bell Dash.

No accounts

These games have no accounts and no sign-in. You do not provide a name, email address, or any other personal information to play.

No data collection

The current builds of these games collect no data. They make no network calls, send nothing to any server, and contain no analytics, no trackers, and no advertising.

Stored only on your device

The only thing any of these games stores is a best score, kept in local storage on your own device:

  • Hangry Settlers stores its best score and a short summary sentence of that best run.
  • Balloon Bringer, Nugget Scout, Hangry Flyer, Settled Steak Snake, and Dinner Bell Dash each store only their best score number.

That stored score never leaves your device. You can remove it at any time by clearing the game's site or app data, or by uninstalling the game.
